05 · Trust & regulatory readiness

Designed for regulated insurance

KGA ARIA and ARIA Assure support insurers in aligning agentic AI with the governance, model-risk, privacy and operational-resilience frameworks that apply across Canada, the United States and the European Union.

Canada
  • OSFI E-23 — Model Risk Management, including AI and machine-learning models
  • OSFI B-10 — Third-Party Risk Management
  • OSFI B-13 — Technology and Cyber Risk Management
  • PIPEDA — Federal privacy
United States
  • NAIC Model Bulletin — Use of Artificial Intelligence Systems by Insurers
  • NAIC AI Principles — Fairness, accountability, transparency and security
  • State insurance departments — AI governance and unfair-discrimination expectations
European Union
  • EU AI Act — Risk management, transparency, human oversight and record-keeping
  • DORA — Digital operational resilience and ICT third-party risk
  • GDPR — Data protection and automated decision-making
